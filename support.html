How does the daily grid work?

Every day a new grid opens at 00:00 UTC, and it's the same grid for every player. It has three rows and three columns, and each one is a category — usually a club, sometimes a country, a position or an era.

Tap a cell and name a footballer who fits both its row and its column. You have nine guesses for nine cells, and each guess is final, right or wrong. A player can only be used once per grid.

Correct answers score points, and rarer answers score more. Difficulty goes from Easy to Legend, and the daily grid plays offline.

Why was my answer rejected, and how do I report it?

An answer is rejected when, according to our data, the player doesn't fit both categories — or when you've already used that player in another cell of the same grid.

Our career data comes from Wikidata and licensed football data. It's good but not perfect: a short spell, a loan or a recent transfer can be missing.

If you're sure you were right, tap This should count on the red banner that appears after the rejection. The report sends us only the player and the two categories, so the dataset can be corrected in an update. You can see what you've reported under Settings → Data → Reported answers.

Reporting doesn't give the guess back. The button doesn't appear when the player was already used — that's a rule, not a data error.

Am I playing against a bot?

Sometimes, and you'll always know. In Live 1v1, if no human opponent turns up within a few seconds, a practice bot takes the game so you're not left waiting. It is always labelled Practice bot on screen.

Bot matches don't change your rating, and bots are never used in anything you pay for or anything with a prize.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings → Store → Coins & Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Restoring brings back Pro. Coins are a consumable: once bought they sit in your in-game balance, and Apple's restore doesn't apply to them.

Pro renews automatically until you cancel it in your App Store subscriptions (iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Payment and refunds are handled by Apple; we can't issue refunds ourselves.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. Your account, profile and scores are removed from our server immediately and permanently.

Progress on this device stays. To clear it as well, use Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app. Deleting the account also loses unspent coins and any remaining subscription time (see the Terms of Use).

The button needs a connection. If you can't use it, email us the nickname shown under Settings → Account → Player and we'll delete the account for you.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Go to Settings → Account → Export my data. The app puts your server account — daily scores and challenge results — and everything it stores on this device into one JSON file, and shows you a summary first.

Nothing leaves your phone until you choose where to send or save the file. If you're offline, the server part is empty.
