The game

FootballGrid is a football trivia game. You may play it for your own entertainment. You may not resell it, or use it to build a competing dataset.

No affiliation

FootballGrid is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any club, league, federation or player. Club and player names are used descriptively, to say which club or player a puzzle is about. Clubs are drawn as abstract two-colour marks in their real colours; no crest, badge, emblem or player image appears anywhere in the app.

Career data is derived from Wikidata (CC0).

Your account

The app creates an anonymous account for you. There is no password, which means there is no way to recover it if you delete the app without keeping the device. That is the trade for never asking who you are.

Do not use the app to interfere with other players: automating play, tampering with the client to forge scores or match results, or exploiting a defect instead of reporting it. We may remove scores or accounts that do.

Practice bots

When no human opponent is available in Live 1v1, you are matched with a practice bot. It is always labelled as one on screen. Bots are never used in anything you pay for and never in anything with a prize.

Purchases

Pro and Remove Ads are sold through Apple. Payment, renewal and refunds are handled by Apple under your App Store account settings; we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

  • Coins are not sold. You earn them only by choosing to watch an optional ad. They are a virtual item with no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or transferred between accounts.
  • Pro renews automatically until you cancel it in App Store settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period.
  • Purchases belong to your Apple ID, not to the game account: Restore purchases brings them back on a new device. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in App Store settings. Unspent coins are lost when you delete your account.

Nothing sold in the app gives an advantage in ranked play.

Content in the game

Everything you see is generated by the app or drawn from the dataset. There is no chat and no free-text field, so there is no user-generated content to moderate — your nickname is generated, not typed.

Availability

The game runs offline. Online features — leaderboards, challenges, live matches — depend on a server that may be unavailable, and may change or be withdrawn.
