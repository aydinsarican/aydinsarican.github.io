What's inside

  • Daily Grid

    One new 3×3 grid every day, the same for everyone. Nine guesses, each one final — and rarer answers score more.

  • Live 1v1

    Take turns claiming cells against a real opponent. Three in a row wins, and every win against a human moves your rating.

  • Career Path

    A career is revealed club by club, one more with every wrong guess. Name the player early for more points.

  • Collection

    Every correct answer unlocks a player card — common, rare or legend. Typographic cards: the name and the career, no photos.

Fair by design

  • No sign-up, no password: an anonymous account and a generated nickname.
  • The daily grid works offline.
  • Practice bots are always labelled and never used in anything you pay for.
  • Nothing sold in the app gives an advantage in ranked play.
  • No crests and no player photos — clubs appear as abstract marks in their colours.
