Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

FootballGrid is a football trivia game. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written against the app's actual data flows, not a template.

The short version

There is no sign-up, no login screen, and no password. You never type your name, your email or anything else about yourself. The game is fully playable offline; the online part only adds leaderboards, challenges and live matches.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, your email address or your advertising identifier. We do not track you across other apps or websites. The app shows ads from Google AdMob; how that works is explained under "Advertising" below.

What is stored on your device

Your progress lives on your device: solved grids, streak, unlocked cards, level, badges, settings and reminders. If you delete the app, this is gone.

What is stored on our server

When the online layer is available, the app creates an anonymous account. It is a random identifier — it is not linked to your Apple ID, your email or your phone.

Against that identifier we store:

  • A generated nickname. The app makes it up from a fixed word list. You do not type it, so it can never contain your real name.
  • Your progress summary: level, experience, card count, and the club you picked as your favourite.
  • Daily grid scores: which day and difficulty you played, how many cells you solved, the points you earned, and the players you named in each cell. The answers are stored because the score is calculated on the server from them.
  • Challenge results: the score you posted to a challenge you or a friend started.
  • Live 1v1 matches: who played, which cells were claimed with which answers, wrong answers, timeouts, and your rating.
  • Reported answers: when you flag "this should count", we store which player and which two categories, so the dataset can be corrected.
  • Coin balance and history: how many coins you have and how each movement happened.

Our server is operated by Supabase and hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Data stays in the EU.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to see which screens are used and to receive crash reports. These are not linked to your account and are not used for advertising. They include a random per-device identifier generated by Firebase.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for coins, and an ad after some finished Live 1v1 matches. Pro and Remove Ads turn the match ad off; the coin video is always optional.

  • We request non-personalised ads and the app never asks for permission to track you, so your advertising identifier is not available to Google or to us.
  • To show, count and protect ads from fraud, Google receives technical information from your device — for example your IP address, device model, operating system version and how you interact with the ad. Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Purchases

Pro and Remove Ads are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details; the app checks on your device, with Apple's signed receipts, which purchases you have. Coins are not sold.

Where the football data comes from

Player and club information is derived from Wikidata (CC0). The app does not contact any third-party data service while you play.

Your rights

Two buttons in Settings → Account do the real thing, not a request form:

  • Export my data produces a single file with everything above — your server account and the raw contents of the app's own storage on this device.
  • Delete account deletes the account and everything attached to it from the server, immediately and permanently. It does not cancel a Pro subscription: subscriptions belong to your Apple ID and are cancelled in your App Store settings.

Under GDPR and KVKK you also have the right to correct your data and to object to processing. Since you cannot enter personal data in the first place, the practical route for both is the delete button.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. There is no separate retention period.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify one.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we store, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
